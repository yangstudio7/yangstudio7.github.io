Privacy Policy for Yangstudio7 Studio

Last updated: 03/08/2026

Yangstudio7 Studio operates the mobile games and applications (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect personal identifiable information directly. However, our games integrate third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

We use third-party ad networks, specifically AppLovin, to serve advertisements in our games. These third-party services may collect device-specific information (such as GAID, IDFA, IP address) to personalize ads.

We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Users in the EU and California have the right to access, restrict, or delete their data through the in-game settings provided by our ad partners.

3. Children's Privacy

Our games are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
